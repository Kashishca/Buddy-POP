Buddy Pop Privacy Policy Effective date: November 23, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Buddy Pop (the “App”), a floating companion app, handles information when you use it. The App is provided by the developer identified below (“we”, “us”, or “our”). 1. Information We Collect Based on the App’s current design and the developer’s description, Buddy Pop does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer. The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or other third-party services. The App does not intentionally collect information about the other apps you use, their content, or your activity within them. 2. Display Over Other Apps Permission Buddy Pop may request Android’s “Display over other apps” permission so that its companion character can appear on top of other apps. This permission is used for the floating companion experience. Buddy Pop does not use this permission to read the content of other apps, capture screenshots, record your screen, or monitor your activity. You can manage or revoke this permission at any time in your Android device settings. If you revoke it, the floating companion feature may not work. 3. Storage and Sharing Buddy Pop does not maintain an account or backend service and, according to its current design, does not store user information. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. 4. Children’s Privacy Buddy Pop is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly maintain personal information about children. 5. Data Security and Retention Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information to us, we do not maintain a personal-information database or retention schedule. Information handled independently by Android or your device is subject to the relevant platform and device-provider policies. 6. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be published at this same URL, with an updated effective date. 7.
